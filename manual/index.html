05

Hardware Management

Hardware assets are categorized into three protocols: 1) IC Cards, 2) POS Terminals, and 3) Solar Units.

Part A: IC Card Provisioning

When provisioning new cards, you MUST enter both the Printed Card Number and the Physical UID simultaneously. This dual-mapping ensures that assets can be efficiently searched and identified using the visible serial number on the card surface during future audits.

Part B: POS Terminal Assignment

To authorize a POS terminal, you must register its SN (Serial Number) and assign it to a specific operator. Each terminal supports a strict 1:1 binding; multiple operator logins on a single device are not permitted.

When the terminal is online, the system automatically verifies its registration status and displays the authorized operator credentials on the device dashboard.

Part C: Solar Unit Provisioning

Solar Home System (SHS) units must be registered in the central registry before deployment. Enter the Machine ID and associated peripheral details to initialize the hardware lifecycle tracking.

Terminal Operations

01

Device Authorization & SN Check

New terminals must be registered in the central system registry to gain access. If the device is not recognized, it will be restricted from entering the operational interface. Action: Ensure the SN is added via the Management Web, then tap the REFRESH button on the terminal.

02

Security Enforcement & Lockout

If the terminal is remotely locked or reconciliation has expired, a Security Lock screen will be displayed. You must contact an Administrator or Finance Officer to authorize an unlock from the backend.

Note: Once unlocked in the system, tap REFRESH on the terminal. An active internet connection is required for this sync.

03

Login & Regional Protocol

To maintain data integrity, each POS terminal is strictly limited to managing customers within its assigned region.

Authorized Operations: 1. New Customer Registration; 2. Hardware Installation; 3. Account Reloading.

04

Data Synchronization Protocol

Upon successful login, the system automatically triggers a background synchronization (internet connection required) to fetch the latest customer whitelist and rates.

Action: You can also manually trigger a full update by tapping the GREEN SYNC button on the dashboard.

05

Hardware Deployment Protocol

To initialize a customer's electricity service, you must complete the hardware binding process. Action: Tap the Device Installation button on the dashboard.

System Logic: This directory only displays customers who have not yet had hardware assigned. You can efficiently locate a specific profile using the Search Bar (supports Name or Customer ID).

06

Machine Mapping & QR Selection

Select the target customer from the list, then assign a specific SHS Machine ID to their profile. Efficiency: The system supports QR Code Scanning to quickly select and verify the machine serial number without manual input.

07

Initial Recharge & Activation

After successfully mapping the hardware, you must enter the Recharge Days. The system will automatically calculate the total amount based on the localized daily rate.

Crucial: If the customer fails to complete the payment and write data to the IC card, the entire installation process is considered void and will be aborted.

08

Subsequent Recharge Protocol

For subsequent recharges of existing customers, select the Recharge function from the terminal dashboard. The operator must first read the customer's IC card to automatically retrieve their bound profile and device information before proceeding with the transaction.

09

Recharge Execution & Card Write Protocol

After verifying customer details, enter the desired Recharge Days. Confirm the calculated total amount and proceed to tap the IC card against the NFC sensor to write the updated validity period.

10

Offline Sync & Network Validation

Since most recharges occur in offline environments, the terminal securely queues transaction data locally. When attempting to upload records, the system validates the network connection. If triggered offline accidentally, a prompt will appear requesting internet access to prevent data corruption.

11

Online Sync Confirmation & Execution

Even when the device is online, initiating a manual data synchronization requires a secondary confirmation to prevent accidental triggers. Once the synchronization process begins, it cannot be interrupted to guarantee data integrity.

12

Special Tools & Advanced Card Operations

Special tools can be permanently authorized for Admin accounts or temporarily granted to Operator accounts. These tools enable advanced technical operations such as formatting/resetting physical IC cards and creating Supercards (Preset Cards) for special deployment scenarios.
